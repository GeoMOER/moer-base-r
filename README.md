# moer-base-r

This introductory R programming course is provided by the Lab of Environmental Informatics (University of Marburg, Germany).
It was funded by the “digLL” initiative of the Hessian Ministry of Higher Education, Research, Science and the Arts.

https://geomoer.github.io/moer-base-r/

Unused lesson sources and templates are preserved in [deprecated/](deprecated/README.md),
with their original paths and checksums recorded for restoration.
